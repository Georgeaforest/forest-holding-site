The company

One of the largest online wine cellars in Greece.

House of Wine (houseofwine.gr) is one of the largest online wine cellars in Greece, with more than 1,000 labels of wine, spirits, beer and delicatessen products.

It offers carefully curated labels from top producers in Greece and around the world, with nationwide delivery.
