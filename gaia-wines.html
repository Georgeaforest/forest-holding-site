The company

Bringing Greek indigenous varieties to the world.

Gaia Wines was founded in 1994 by oenologist Yiannis Paraskevopoulos and agronomist Pantelis Karatsalos, with the aim of bringing Greek indigenous grape varieties to the world.

It operates two wineries — on Santorini, home of Assyrtiko, and in Koutsi, Nemea, land of Agiorgitiko. Wines such as Thalassitis and Gaia Estate are exported to more than 25 countries.
