01 — About us

A group with a long-term approach.

Forest Holding is a Greek holding company managing a diversified portfolio of businesses across strategic sectors of the economy. Our aim is to create sustainable value for shareholders, employees and local communities.

With a focus on transparency, corporate governance and long-term stability, we support the group's companies so they can grow responsibly and consistently.

02 — Our philosophy

Patient capital, like a forest.

A forest is not built; it is grown. It rewards those who plant early, tend consistently and resist the urge to harvest too soon. That is how we think about the companies we invest in.

“We don't look for the next quarter. We look for businesses with character, quality and an authentic Greek identity — and we stay.”
Select

Healthy businesses first

Sound fundamentals, strong teams and products people already love. We invest in what works, then help it work better.

Support

Modernise, don't replace

Governance, systems, digital tools and capital — brought in alongside the people who built the business.

Stay

A horizon of decades

No exit clock. Value compounds when ownership is stable and decisions are made for the long run.
