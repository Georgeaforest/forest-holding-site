01 — About us

A group with a long-term approach.

FOREST HOLDING is a Greek holding company managing a diversified portfolio of businesses across strategic sectors of the economy. Our aim is to create sustainable value for shareholders, employees and local communities.

With a focus on transparency, corporate governance and long-term stability, we support the group's companies so they can grow responsibly and consistently.

  • Endurance

    We invest with a horizon of decades, not quarters.

  • Transparency

    Clear processes and responsible corporate governance.

  • Partnership

    We support teams, we do not replace them.
