Aigaion Loukoumades

Loukoumades in the heart of Athens, since 1926.

The company

“Aigaion Loukoumades” is a historic Athens shop that has been serving loukoumades since 1926, on Panepistimiou Street.

With almost a century of roots and respect for tradition, the business is being renewed and returns with a modern touch, keeping its authentic flavour.
