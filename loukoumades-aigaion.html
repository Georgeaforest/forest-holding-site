The company

A historic Athens shop, renewed.

“Aigaion Loukoumades” is a historic Athens shop that has been serving loukoumades since 1926, in the historic centre of the city.

With almost a century of roots and respect for tradition, the business is being renewed and returns with a modern touch, keeping its authentic flavour.
